I am a first-year PhD student in the Decision, Risk, and Operations division at Columbia University, where I am fortunate to work with Prof. Hongseok Namkoong and Prof. Jing Dong. Before my PhD, I spent two years at Google from 2024 to 2026 working on embeddings, post-training, and agents. I did my undergraduate studies in computer science at Middlebury College and Columbia.

I am interested in principled and useful ideas that draw from machine learning, operations research, and statistics. I also go by Albert.
